# Contextual view of a consumer-produced authority judgment

Parsing is a declaration, not permission. authorityCardAnswer compares current consumer context and explicit clock before accepting a judgment through a receiver-selected verification port. It never calculates allow/ask/deny from grants.

Reuse: `normalizeRegistryHorizon`, `existing consumer authority evaluation`.

commits: place/snapshot, key/authority/coverage, policy/budget/demands, grants/retirements, catalog/standingContract, explicit horizon and issuedAt, request-digest decisions, external judgment evidence

Consumer obligations:

- Verify the actual judge, signature/coverage, every statement/context binding and accepted clock source.
- Supply current authority identity after withdrawal, even when content snapshot is unchanged.

Caller supplies byte and per-collection item budgets; no ambient clock, I/O, default verifier or universal region list.

Two receiver fixtures verify signature plus a fixed judgment statement. This is not a live production authority fold.

Contract meaning only. Admission, production consumer verification and public delivery require their separate acta.
