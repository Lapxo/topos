# topos-profile-composition/1

Describe finite parent-by-digest profiles, declared field contracts and unambiguous category pins.

- Parent bytes are supplied by caller, checked by digest, decoded canonically and traversed under an explicit item/closure budget; no I/O or universal region list.
- Missing parents remain needs/bytes; corrupted bytes refuse integrity; cycles refuse.
- Each field names its semantic contract digest; receiver selects the PolicyField with matching coordinate. Uncatalogued semantics refuse.
- Existing meet/order classifies narrowing or widening. Widening and non-meet replacement leave authorize-transition needs; adding a field is addition, never called narrowing.
- A witness remains a reading declaration requiring verify-witness; attest never grants authority. A witness does not erase authorize-transition needs.
- Category pins name category, class, algorithm and digest. Ambiguous inherited/own pins refuse instead of last-wins.
- All ancestor constraints remain visible; inherited incompatible non-meet declarations are not silently overridden. No inverse of a reduced aggregate is reconstructed.

## Consumer obligations

- Witness signature/CI provenance and consuming policy acceptance
- Authorization and native admission of widening or added permissions
- Resolving incompatible inherited profiles or replacing category pins

The native vector captures positive and negative declarations at catalog/items and orchard/harvest. Parsing or calculation establishes no authority, admission, stored effect or release approval.
