# Consumed algebra compatibility

Topos 0.2.2 consumes Obligations 0.4.5 exactly: archive `sha256:db4d1ed06ecd8e5413feb2f79b846d4cec98ad12ac44ae3c3d96a35fe261b2bb`. Its 307 installed members match the published archive. The existing Topos implementation and exports are unchanged; this release changes the consumed dependency and release metadata, without changing the wire, algebra or authority operations.

The compatibility vector retains the actual Obligations 0.4.4 baseline and records four native executions against 0.4.5: a positive and a named refusal on each of the reservoir and shipping places. The reader delegates parsing, meet and output to the selected interval form. Inputs, reader bytes and projections have declared digests. Diagnostics replace absolute fixture locations with explicit placeholders; original transcript digests are retained. These are two places under one origin, not independent origins or a universal equivalence theorem. Historical receipts keep their original identities.

The conformance reader checks the declared contract, samples and captured vector. Missing or altered inputs refuse. Its inherited regions, assertions and numerical limits are retained; receipts for the replacement implementation are regenerated rather than relabeled.

This contract establishes compatibility only for the consumed surface and declared vectors. It does not authorize release signatures or establish second-machine portability. Measure and aggregation profiles are separate contracts. The demands `conditional-lineage-identification`, `causal-subgroup-recovery` and `oracle-free-reference` remain open.
