# topos-portable-birth/1

Describe a bounded public birth payload without transferring admission or local authority.

- Origin digest plus canonical unsigned alphabet writes; byte and item budgets supplied by caller.
- Only declared public-line fields are permitted; by/sig/epoch envelopes and authority/key/root/store/derived material are refused.
- No filesystem, store, signer, root, historical admission or automatic installation is part of the profile.
- Occupied coordinates produce named open pack/collision needs, never replacement.
- Arbitrary data cannot be certified free of secrets by syntax: the public-data producer remains responsible for selection; the receiver never executes or lands these lines by inspection.
- All original declaration bytes are preserved; incompatible declarations remain for the native consumer to judge.

## Consumer obligations

- Origin authentication
- Consumer collision resolution and native coverage/admission
- Public-data selection; no generic secret-detection claim

The native vector captures positive and negative declarations at catalog/items and orchard/harvest. Parsing or calculation establishes no authority, admission, stored effect or release approval.
